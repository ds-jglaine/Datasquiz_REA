## REA-SERVICE-SCAFFOLD-01 handover

Source-only scaffold for the standalone REA service. No install, no unit, no
deploy, no edge route and no admission was performed by this story.

Delivered surface: `server.js` plus `api/service.js` (readiness and splash
only, every other path 404), the two `.datasquiz` contract files,
`deployment/operations.json`, the non-root Script 0-4 set, `docs/rea-service.md`
and the service suite under `tests/service/` run by `npm run test:service`.

Readback evidence in the handover comment: five kill mutations, the loopback
`/health` `/` `/mcp` status codes and the baseline TAP log.
